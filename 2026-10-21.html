Cyberpunk 2077 Update 2.5 lands tomorrow, and the details have already leaked

CD Projekt Red's free Edgerunners 2 Update (2.5) arrives on 22 Oct to tie in with the anime's second season, which hit Netflix yesterday. The official tease promises cyberware customisation and a new dual-wielding playstyle. A content creator's video went up early before being pulled, and according to reports it shows dual-wield pistols, a new Edgerunners side quest, a new radio station, a field-of-view slider on consoles and mod.io support (though reportedly not on Switch 2). The full reveal is in a REDstream today at 17:00 SAST.

PS5PCHelldivers 2

Helldivers 2 Update 7.2.0 is out, with a big weapon balance pass

The one we were waiting on arrived alongside yesterday's Scope of Authority Warbond. The M90A shotgun goes from 605 to 726 damage, the Orbital Airburst Strike cooldown drops from 100 to 90 seconds, the Maxigun no longer locks your movement after firing, and there are balance passes on the Killzone and Halo Legendary Warbond weapons, the Sickle and several stratagems. On PC, the game has also swapped GameGuard for Easy Anti-Cheat.

Xbox

Report: Xbox handheld in 2027, Project Helix console in 2028, and more job cuts

Fortune says a first-party Xbox handheld is due by the 2027 holiday season, and the Helix console in 2028, with an internal price projection of $1,200 to $1,800 unless Microsoft subsidises it. The same report says CEO Asha Sharma has cut 1,868 jobs so far out of 3,200 planned, with more cuts this month, in November and in January. Treat it as a report, not an announcement.
