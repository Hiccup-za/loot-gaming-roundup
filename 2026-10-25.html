Wo Long 2 will widen parry windows and boost spells after alpha demo feedback

Koei Tecmo and Team Ninja have published the results of September's Wings of Ember alpha demo survey and a long list of changes for the full game. Deflect and Insight Skill timing windows get wider, successful Insight Skills restore more Spirit, Martial Arts and Wizardry Spells get stronger, and you'll be able to cast some spells and switch weapons in mid-air. Performance should improve too, with a steadier frame rate, faster fast-travel and retry loading, less pop-in and better lighting in dark areas. Dying inside the Phantom Realm will also let you restart from within it.

Survival horror RPG Look Outside comes to PS5 and Switch on 30 Oct

Devolver Digital is bringing the acclaimed PC game (83 on Metacritic) to PS5, PS4, Switch 2 and Switch just in time for Halloween. It's set in a single apartment building where anyone who looks outside is transformed, and mixes Resident Evil-style resource management with turn-based combat. It takes about 10 to 15 hours, so it's a good weekend game.

Co-op farming and crafting game Luma Island hits PS5 and Xbox on 2 Dec

Feel Free Games' farming adventure RPG costs $19.99 and adds full cross-platform play for up to four players across PS5, Xbox Series and PC, so you can farm, craft and explore together. If you like the crafting loop in Valheim or Palworld but want something cosier, it's one to watch.

Xbox COO Helen Chiang leaves Microsoft after just three months in the role

According to an internal memo obtained by The Verge, Xbox CEO Asha Sharma told staff that Chiang was leaving Microsoft. She had been promoted in July to Xbox's first chief operating officer, after 21 years at Microsoft that included leading Mojang and Minecraft. Her duties are being split among other Xbox leaders for now.

A free, officially licensed Knightmare game is in the works

The classic ITV dungeon show is getting a hand-drawn point-and-click adventure for PC next year to mark its 40th anniversary, with voice acting from the original cast, including Hugo Myatt as Treguard. It will take one or two hours to finish and there are no console plans.
