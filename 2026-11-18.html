🏆 GTA VI leads The Game Awards 2026 nominations with eight

Rockstar's game is up for Game of the Year two days before it launches, alongside 007 First Light, Control Resonant, Phantom Blade Zero, Resident Evil Requiem and Zelda: Ocarina of Time. Nintendo is the most-nominated publisher with 12 nods, including Pokémon Pokopia twice. For your games, The Witcher 3 is up for Best Ongoing Game and Best Community Support, Helldivers 2 for Best Community Support, The Blood of Dawnwalker for Best RPG and Best Narrative, and Pokémon Champions for Best Mobile Game. The nominations also confirmed Manni L. Perez as Lucia. Fan voting is open until 9 December, and the show airs on 10 December.

SourceEurogamer ↗Nintendo Life ↗

💿 GTA VI won't run until you install the latest PS5 system update

PlayStation Support says firmware 14.23.00 is required to play GTA VI and should be installed before midnight on 19 November, when the game unlocks. It's a 1.3GB download. Sony normally gives a grace period before an update becomes mandatory, but it's tightening security after recent PS5 hacks.

🧛 The Blood of Dawnwalker's New Game+ update is out now

Update 1.2 has landed a few days early on PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PC. New Game+ keeps your level, skills and inventory, raises the level cap to 100, scales every region to you, and adds NG+ gear with new stats. It also tweaks health pools for level 17 to 30 enemies and fixes terrain geometry. The Xbox version is 20% off in the Black Friday sale.

🥊 Pokémon Champions gets a balance patch on 2 December

The update gives new moves to several Pokémon, including Play Rough for all the Eeveelutions and Roost for Beedrill and Hydreigon. Screech, Steel Wing, Metal Sound and Frost Breath become fully accurate, Rock Slide gets more accurate but flinches less often, and Flash Fire and Stalwart are reworked.

🏴‍☠️ Sea of Thieves almost halves its install size on Thursday

Rare's 19 November update cuts the game from over 100GB to 56.4GB on Xbox Series X, and to about 25GB on Series S and Xbox One. PS5 and PC also get smaller installs, alongside bug fixes. Handy if you're making room for GTA VI.

Firaxis lays off a third of its staff as studio head Heather Hazen leaves

Bloomberg reports that around 60 people have lost their jobs at the 2K-owned Civilization and XCOM studio. 2K says Firaxis is "restructuring and recentering" around strategy games. Bloomberg also reports that a new XCOM has struggled and missed a milestone over the summer, though development continues.

PS5

Sony beats the £2 billion UK lawsuit over PlayStation Store prices

The Competition Appeal Tribunal rejected Alex Neill's class action for about 12.2 million players, which claimed Sony shut out competition and charged "excessive and unfair" prices. The tribunal found Sony competes in a wider market with Xbox, Nintendo and PC, and that buyers can make informed choices when they pick a console.

SourceEurogamer ↗

PlayStation has no Game of the Year nominee for the first time since 2014

Sony-published games had been nominated every year since Bloodborne in 2015, and three of them won. This year Saros, Marvel Tokon and Wolverine all missed the shortlist, though PlayStation still has nine nominations across other categories.
