🪓 Valheim patch 1.0.30 makes failed Forge of Potential upgrades far less painful

Iron Gate's latest patch makes the Forge of Potential return substantially more materials when an upgrade fails, rebalances idol drop rates so they turn up more evenly, and stops seeding its RNG on character names. It also fixes players getting stuck in portals in multiplayer, adds a 'Brighten dark environment' accessibility setting and music in the Sunken Crypt. It affects crossplay, so everyone in your group needs to update before connecting.

Unreal Engine 6 will drop Blueprints and the Actor Gameplay Framework

Epic's EVP of Development Marcus Wassmer says UE6 will be Verse-first on the new Scene Graph framework, reversing June's promise that Blueprints and Actors would coexist. Epic expects AI to make converting UE5 projects practical, saying it ported Fortnite Battle Royale's 20,000-plus Blueprints to about 3 million lines of Verse in a prototype.

SourceGamesIndustry.biz ↗

CD Projekt says The Witcher 4 launches on PS5, Xbox and PC at the same time

After this week's Sony marketing deal, joint CEO Michał Nowakowski said The Witcher 4 and its other upcoming games stay multiplatform: "All the platforms release at the same time – PS5, XB, and PC. No one gets left behind." Switch 2 wasn't mentioned, and the game is still slated for 2028.
