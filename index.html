PS5PCHelldivers 2

Arrowhead: no Helldivers 3 unless it's very different from Helldivers 2

Chief creative officer Johan Pilestedt told Rock Paper Shotgun that 95 per cent of Arrowhead is still working on Helldivers 2, with only a small team exploring something new. A third game would only make sense if it were different enough for both to exist side by side, he says, because "the biggest competitor to a Helldivers 3 would be Helldivers 2". So expect plenty more Helldivers 2 updates rather than a sequel any time soon.

Switch 2Cult of the Lamb

Cult of the Lamb is out on Switch 2, and it's a free upgrade

The Switch 2 Edition is now on the eShop (about 3.8GB) with enhanced resolution, improved frame rates and GameShare support. The Woolhaven expansion and all DLC packs come along too, and Switch 1 owners upgrade for free. The Switch version is currently $9.99 or your regional equivalent.

PS5Switch 2XboxPCCall of Duty

Modern Warfare 4 multiplayer is live early, but first impressions are rough

Infinity Ward unlocked competitive multiplayer earlier than planned, so the full game is now playable. The campaign and DMZ have gone down well, but fan site Charlie Intel called multiplayer a "mess", citing weak maps, spawns that are still broken after the beta, and heavy skill-based matchmaking. Patches usually help, so it may be worth waiting a week if multiplayer is your main reason to buy.

Report: Arkane has until 13 Nov to find a buyer or close

Le Monde reports that the Dishonored studio, which Microsoft plans to cut, is exploring several kinds of takeover, with over 200 jobs at risk. Its union says Marvel's Blade is virtually finished. Microsoft says it is "actively seeking a partner" to finish and release the game.

Xbox

Report: Netflix offered $500 million for Xbox film and TV rights, and Xbox asked for $800 million

Puck says Netflix wanted a seven-year exclusive on adaptations of Xbox games; the deal fell through and Xbox is now said to be pursuing smaller deals with more partners. Xbox says it has had "record-breaking interest" in its games and still has Minecraft and Diablo animated series coming with Netflix.

Shigeru Miyamoto will be the first game developer to receive Japan's Order of Culture

The Emperor presents the award on Culture Day, 3 Nov. Miyamoto said he was "a little embarrassed", since games are made by many people, but was glad to see games recognised as culture.
