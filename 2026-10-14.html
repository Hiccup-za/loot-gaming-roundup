Witcher 3 Remastered: PS5 hotfix 5.01b

CD Projekt Red pushed a ~500MB stability hotfix (update 5.000.500) for The Witcher 3 Remastered on PS5 and PS5 Pro. There are no itemised notes beyond "various stability improvements", and MP1st says some players still report crashes. Patch 5.01 for Switch 2 is still in the works with no date.

PS5PCHelldivers 2

Helldivers 2: Scope of Authority Warbond on 20 Oct

Arrowhead's next Premium Warbond is all about long range: the R-40 Sightliner bolt-action sniper, P-44 Mist machine pistol, backpack-fed AMW-20 Apex anti-materiel rifle, LAS-44 Nova charge rifle and G-11 Caltrops. Both new armours carry the Stationary Shroud passive, which cuts sway and detection range while crouched or prone. It's separate from Update 7.2, which still has no date.

SourcePlayStation Blog ↗

GTA 6 on PC in early 2027, report says

GamesBeat, citing four industry sources, reports GTA 6 will hit PC within the first three months of 2027. Rockstar hasn't commented. The console launch on PS5 and Xbox Series is still 19 Nov.

Modern Warfare 4 early access dates

Activision's Battle.net post confirms Campaign Early Access from Friday 16 Oct for digital pre-orders. DMZ reportedly opens to pre-order players on 20 Oct, and Infinity Ward says it won't have a solo PvE mode, though you can switch off squad fill. Full launch is 23 Oct.

Boltgun 2 is out today

Auroch Digital's retro Warhammer 40K shooter launches today with two playable characters (Ultramarine Malum Caedo and Sister of Battle Nyra Veyrath) and a branching campaign. The PlayStation Store lists unlock at 16:00 UTC (18:00 SAST) and £19.99 in the UK. A Switch 2 version comes later.

SourceAuroch Digital ↗Steam ↗
Xbox

Xbox forms Xbox Labs

Xbox CEO Asha Sharma told staff that CTO Scott Van Vliet will lead a small team exploring things like AI-driven decompiles, ports to new hardware and browser versions of games, after a wave of fan-made AI ports. She said Xbox will build in the open and share its tools with other developers.

SourceVGC ↗Xbox Wire (UK) ↗
