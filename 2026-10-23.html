The leaked Mass Effect trailer is real, and shows what BioWare was making before it closed

A day after EA shut BioWare, an in-engine trailer for the cancelled next Mass Effect turned up on the series subreddit. Bloomberg's Jason Schreier and IGN have both confirmed it's genuine. The title card calls it Mass Effect Universe: it would have returned to the Milky Way thousands of years after Mass Effect 3, with Liara back. It was still very early, made by a small team using pre-production assets, and may have been meant for N7 Day on 7 Nov. Separately, Amazon's Mass Effect TV series is reportedly still in production.

CapcomResident Evil

Capcom data breach hits more than 11.7 million Capcom ID accounts

Exposed data includes email addresses, last-login IP addresses and account creation dates; 43 cases also leaked birth dates, gender, region and nicknames. Capcom says card details weren't affected and it has reset 196 passwords that were changed. If you have a Capcom ID, now's a good time to change your password.

Wolverine tops US sales in September as PS5 outsells Switch 2

Circana says Marvel's Wolverine was September's best-seller, with Sony's biggest launch-month dollar sales since Helldivers 2. PS5 hardware spending rose 43% year on year (units up 22%), while Switch 2 fell 12% and Xbox Series 2%. Xbox console unit sales hit an all-time September low.

Report: Xbox plans a first-party handheld for 2027 and the Helix console for 2028

Fortune reports a next-gen handheld "by the 2027 holiday season" after internal clashes over whether it should run trimmed-down Windows or Xbox's own software, with Xbox now largely steering the OS. Microsoft hasn't announced either device's timing.
