Valor Mortis is out today

The Ghostrunner team's Napoleonic first-person soulslike launches today on PS5, Xbox Series, Steam and Day One Game Pass. You rise as a fallen Grande Armée soldier with supernatural powers; unlock is 17:00 UTC (19:00 SAST). New Game+ is planned for later this year.

Pokémon UNITE: Flareon & Jolteon

Flareon (All-Rounder) and Jolteon (Speedster) join Unite Battles today from 4PM JST. The Eevee Festival runs through 1 Dec with a special Appeal-o-rama quick battle — Flareon's license is free during the event.
