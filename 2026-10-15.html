RuneScape: Dragonwilds 1.0.1 is live with save fixes

Jagex's "Eye on Ashenfall" update finally lands the promised fixes for disappearing world saves, plus a catch-up for corrupted characters that drops you back in with aggregated stats and Tier 4 gear. Jagex says a very small number of players may still hit other save issues. It also adds a "Death From Above" jump attack, new ranged perks like Ammo Recovery and Low Blow (leg shots slow enemies), and bumps the inventory from 32 to 40 slots.

PS5PCEnshrouded

Enshrouded 1.0 launches today on PS5 and PC

Keen Games' co-op survival-crafting RPG leaves early access and debuts on PS5 today. 1.0 opens the full Embervale map, adds the new Cinder's Cradle biome (lava wastes below, floating islands above), full voice acting and an ending to the current story. Keen's press release puts launch at 16:00 CEST, which is 16:00 SAST, though some reports say the PS Store listing shows a later unlock. It's $39.99 with 10% off for the first 14 days. Xbox follows in spring 2027.

PS5XboxGTA

Rockstar opens up about GTA 6's long development

In a Bloomberg feature, Rockstar confirmed GTA 6 started as the over-ambitious "Project Americas" and cited the difficulty of defining realism, complex AI, remote-work limits and a three-way leadership structure. It denied a reported $1.5 billion budget without giving the real number, and technical lead Phil Hooker said the new engine and tools should make future games faster. Launch is still 19 Nov.

SourcePolygon ↗The Verge ↗
PS5XboxPCDiablo IV

Diablo IV patch fixes the Prime Evil quest bug

Blizzard's small update stops characters using a Splinter of a Prime Evil before finishing the Seasonal questline, which locked them out of Prime Evil Powers. Already-bugged characters aren't reset, so the workaround is to finish the questline on a new character in the same partition. It also fixes Horadric Cube rerolls for some affixes and a few boss and loot bugs.

SourceMP1st ↗
