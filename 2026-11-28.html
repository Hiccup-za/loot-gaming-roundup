🦋 Hollow Knight: Silksong's free Sea of Sorrow expansion arrives on 11 December

Team Cherry used Friday's Cherry Time broadcast to date Silksong's first expansion, which is free for everyone who owns the game. Hornet voyages above and beneath Pharloom's forgotten ocean, across islands, forts and deep caverns, with new bosses, more Christopher Larkin music, cannon-launching traversal and even silk-fishing. Team Cherry calls it "unlike anything we've done before", and it looks far bigger than any of Hollow Knight's free DLCs.

⚡ Pokémon GO links up with Pokémon Champions from 8 December

A week after Champions' 2 December update, bringing Pokémon you befriended in GO to Champions as visitors earns a one-off Professor Willow Outfit Set and up to 10 Training Tickets between 8 and 18 December, on Switch and mobile. GO runs a Champions Celebration Event from 8 to 14 December with Charmander, Starly, Grookey, Absol and Gible in the wild and more Kecleon at PokéStops.

🪐 Warframe's Tau update on 9 December is "practically Warframe 2", says Digital Extremes

Tau adds a whole second solar system with a roughly two-hour story quest, a new navigation system and two regions at launch: the hazard-filled World-Seeder Oasis and the ringed city of Fornax with three hubs. Spy missions and Steel Path return, there's a card game, an on-rails driving minigame between hubs and a no-combat Night Walk mode where you walk a dog.

SourceEurogamer ↗

🐝 Yooka-Laylee and the Impossible Lair's Definitive Edition skips Xbox because of cost

Playtonic says technical challenges and costs mean the 15 December upgrade, with 120fps, reworked lighting and the Trowzer's Top Tonic Pack DLC, is coming only to PS5, Switch 2 and PC for the foreseeable future. Digital Foundry's John Linneman points out the Xbox One X version already runs at native 4K.

🎭 Rayman Legends Retold aims for 60fps on Switch 2, but the Mario and Luigi costumes are gone

Creative director Loïc Gounon says Ubisoft built the Switch 2 version in parallel with the others and gave it "extra love", targeting 60fps docked and handheld, with GameShare for the Kung Foot mode. The Wii U-era plumber outfits won't return, though other surprise costumes are promised. It's out on 3 December.

🐉 Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World explains why Debora isn't a playable heroine

Producer Takuma Shiraishi says Debora was considered alongside Bianca and Nera, but Yuji Horii narrowed it to two because she first appeared in the DS remake, not the SNES original. She still plays a big part in the story as her younger self. The game is out on 3 December.

🚀 Space RPG Exodus reveals PC specs ahead of its 7 April launch

The Mass Effect-like RPG needs an i5-10400 or Ryzen 5 3600, an RTX 2060 or RX 6600 and 16GB of RAM for 1080p at 30fps, an RTX 3080 or RX 7700 XT for 1440p at 60fps, and an RTX 5080 or RX 9070 XT with 32GB for 4K at 60fps. Plan for 110GB of SSD space.

SourceRock Paper Shotgun ↗

Story of Seasons and Rune Factory maker Marvelous offers early retirement to cut 65 jobs

Marvelous will accept voluntary resignations from full-time and contract staff between 14 December and 8 January, with a lump-sum payout and job-hunting support, as it restructures its console and PC division. It says larger projects and longer development cycles are changing the market, and it will lean more on external partners.
