⚔️ Lords of the Fallen II is now due in April 2027, with an exact date coming in December

CI Games and Hexworks say the Soulslike sequel will launch in April 2027 on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Switch 2 and PC via Steam and the Epic Games Store, after a lengthy delay. The final release date will be announced next month. It's set centuries after the 2023 game, in the last kingdom left standing against the Umbral darkness.

SourceGematsu ↗Eurogamer ↗

🔥 Pokémon Champions adds Megas, Mega Tatsugiri and 24 new Pokémon on 2 December

Regulation Set M-D starts on Wednesday 2 December at 6pm PST (4am SAST on Thursday 3 December) and runs until 3 March 2027. Everything already eligible for Ranked Battles stays in, joined by Heatran, Dondozo, Tatsugiri and more, with 24 newly added Pokémon and four new Mega Evolutions in total.

SourceNintendo Life ↗

🏎️ Mario Kart World 1.9.0 adds two Knockout Tour rallies, eight remixed courses and replays

Feather Rally and Lucky Cat Rally join Knockout Tour once you've played one of its rallies. Eight new course variants arrive for VS Race, Time Trials and online and wireless play, including Airship Fortress 2, DK Pass 2, Koopa Troopa Beach 2 and Wario Stadium 2. Replay is now on the top menu, and races and battles are saved automatically.

SourceGematsu ↗

📉 Digital Foundry finds GTA VI runs better on Xbox Series X than on a base PS5

Digital Foundry compared base PS5, PS5 Pro, Series X and Series S and found the base PS5 has substantially more frequent frame rate drops in busy traffic and big fights than Series X, which also gets 120Hz VRR to smooth out minor dips. Its verdict: Series X is meaningfully better off, despite Sony's Plays Best on PS5 marketing.

SourcePush Square ↗

🧙 Witch life-sim Witchbrook has gone gold for its 28 January launch

Chucklefish and Robotality say the witch-school life-sim and social RPG has gone gold on all platforms, so it's locked in for 28 January 2027. On Switch it launches on the eShop first, with a physical version to follow, and it supports crossplay between Switch 1 and Switch 2.

SourceNintendo Life ↗

🌊 Open-world platformer Demon Tides sails onto PS5, Switch 2 and Switch on 21 January

Fabraz's ocean-hopping 3D platformer, out on PC since February, runs at 2K and 60fps on PS5, and Switch 2 gets a 60fps performance mode or a 30fps high-fidelity mode. Every console version launches on update 1.15 with New Game+ and Randomizer modes, and the graffiti system works across platforms. PS5 and Switch 2 physical editions follow later in 2027.

SourceGematsu ↗

GTA VI knocked Call of Duty's daily players down 42%, Alinea estimates

Alinea Analytics says Call of Duty HQ's daily active users across PlayStation, Xbox and Steam fell from an average of 7.5 million to 4.4 million when GTA VI launched on 19 November, a 44% drop on PlayStation. Four days on it was still 41% below its baseline, while Fortnite and FC 27 took a hit but are already recovering.

SourceEurogamer ↗

CD Projekt signs a deal with Sony that covers The Witcher 4's marketing

CD Projekt's CFO told investors the new arrangement with Sony covers its games on PlayStation and in PS Plus, plus collaboration on the upcoming Witcher 4 marketing campaign. He didn't say whether the marketing will be exclusive.

SourcePush Square ↗Rock Paper Shotgun ↗

Ex-Respawn studio Gravity Well goes into hibernation

After its funding fell through in the spring and 40 jobs went in layoffs last month, the studio of former Titanfall and Apex Legends developers has paused for the foreseeable future. It had been working on an unannounced game for about six years and says it still believes in the IP.

SourceRock Paper Shotgun ↗
